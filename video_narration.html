Point at the intermediate output:
the cited chunk → the printed novel terms → the support score with the novelty penalty visible → the verdict: WEAK.
Say: "This is a claim that reads like it might be true — but the verifier scores each sentence against its cited chunk: cosine times one minus zero-point-six times novelty. The words it needs never appear in the chunk, the penalty kicks in, and the verdict is weak. That's the system finding its own limitation, live."
python -m verirag ask "probiotics prevent antibiotic-associated diarrhea"
